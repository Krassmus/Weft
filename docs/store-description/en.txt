An elearning module is a presentation with interactive elements. You can hand them over to your students or put them on your website as a MOOC.

• Create rich presentations with animations and transitions.
• Add interactive elements like quizzes, buttons, interactive videos, fill-in-the-words, audio, sorting puzzles, image galleries and revealers to your presentation to make it a full elearning module.
• Branching learning paths, jump slides that react to the learning progress of the user.
• Works with LaTeX formulas, websites in iframes, code blocks and other stuff that you won't find in PowerPoint or Keynote.
• Work together with friends and colleagues on your material online or in any cloud storage.
• Rich examples you can learn from and reuse as a template.
• All Weft material is HTML at the end. Works on any computer with a web browser. Can be shared with the whole world.
• Fully open source and free. No accounts needed. We respect your privacy.
• Perfect for schools, universities or corporate training courses.

Weft is an open source authoring tool for presentations and interactive elearning modules. It exports them as HTML or can be played with any other Weft app.
