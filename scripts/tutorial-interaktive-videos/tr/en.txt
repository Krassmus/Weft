6|The video element
7|Actually, the video in Weft is an element for presentations: drag a file onto the slide, press play – done.
8|It knows nothing about what else happens on the slide.
9|But it can do more
10|Start the video. It stops by itself in the middle – and a question appears.

15|HOW IT WORKS
16|Step 1: Create the quiz
17|First create the elements: with “+” above the slide you add a quiz.
18|Write the question and the answers into it and give the quiz a name.
19|Here: Quiz
20|Step 2: The time mark
21|Select the video and open “Configure time marks”. Play the video up to the place you want and create a time mark there.
22|The time mark is here: 0:06
23|The video stops here
24|Step 3: The quiz appears there
25|In the same dialog you pick the quiz under “Makes appear”. Until the video reaches the time mark, it stays invisible.
26|The quiz appears here
27|… and goes away when the video goes on
28|Step 4: The video goes on
29|In the event graph you click “Start of the video”. There you set when the video starts again: after the event “Quiz submitted”.
30|With a short delay – so you can first see whether the answer was right.
31|The video goes on after this event
32|Waiting time in seconds
33|TIME MARKS
34|Time marks in detail
35|Every time mark has a time in minutes and seconds – accurate to about a quarter of a second. You can set as many as you like; Weft sorts them.
36|“Stop the video here” stops the video. Without the tick it keeps playing, and the time mark only triggers something – a hint, for example.

41|EVENT GRAPH
42|The event graph
43|The event graph shows what happens when. With “Next” we go through it once, step by step.
44|The video reaches the time mark 0:06 and stops.
45|At the same moment the quiz appears.
46|From 2 to 3 it goes on as soon as the question is submitted – no matter whether the answer is right or wrong.
47|Then the video starts again, after a waiting time of 1.8 seconds.
48|The video goes on.
49|And the quiz disappears again. For every further question this pattern repeats.
50|TIP
51|Several questions: create first
52|With several questions in the video, first create all the elements and name them well, so you can tell the quizzes apart. Only then set the time marks and assign the elements.

58|IDEAS
